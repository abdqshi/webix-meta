Webix Data Deletion Instructions

Users may request deletion of information associated with their interactions with Webix Automation.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to: webixjo@gmail.com

Use the subject: Data Deletion Request

Please include:

  • Your Instagram username
  • A brief statement requesting deletion of your data
