Webix Automation Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Webix Automation is used to help manage communications and interactions with the Webix Instagram business account.

Information We Process

When users interact with Webix through Instagram, the application may process information provided by Instagram, including account identifiers, message content, comments, and related interaction data.

How We Use Information

Information is processed only for purposes such as receiving messages, responding to customer inquiries, managing comments, and automating customer support interactions.

Data Sharing

Data may be processed through infrastructure and service providers used to operate the automation system. We do not sell personal information.

Data Retention

Information is retained only when necessary for operating, maintaining, and improving the service or when required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their interactions with Webix. Please see our Data Deletion Instructions.
